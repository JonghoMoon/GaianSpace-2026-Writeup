Dear Babb Ceresné,

Thank you for choosing AgriLink. From your first clear morning, your mast will
receive a fresh tile of your farm from the latest satellite pass, and your
Johnripper fleet will pick it up from the mast automatically.

What is in a tile
-----------------
Each tile carries four channels: blue, green, red and near-infrared. Your
tablet app combines blue, green and red into a true-colour picture, just like
a photograph from above.

Delivery stamp
--------------
Every tile carries an AgriLink delivery stamp. You can find it in the bottom
left corner of the tile view in the tablet app. Please have the stamp ready
whenever you contact support: we cannot trace a delivery without it.

Map integrity
-------------
Johnripper tractors check every tile before they build a fertiliser map. If a
tile fails the check, the cab shows "map integrity: unverified" and the tractor
keeps its previous map. Do not override this.
