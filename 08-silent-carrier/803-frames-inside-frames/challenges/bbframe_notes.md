# Frame manual, after the coffee

The page that named the outer wrapper is a brown stain. Pell wrote “start where you started last night” across the stain. He is thinking of the mailbox. This is not the mailbox.

What the dry half of the page still says:

A long frame arrives in pieces. One mark means “this piece begins a frame.” The other pieces only continue it. Join them before you believe the length written on the front.

The front of a frame carries a short check over its own introduction. If that check is wrong, you are not standing on a frame.

Inside the frame the letters travel in their own little packets. A long letter is split across two of them and finishes with a seal over the whole letter, not over one half. A seal that fails is the faded twin. It can still look like words. It is not the letter that was sent.

A drawing number, 270, is legible in the ash. Pell says it is a figure number from the manual. It may be. It may also be the only channel that is actually busy.
