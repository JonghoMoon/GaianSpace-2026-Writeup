# Weather-bird handbook, page missing the top corner

Somebody on Meridian Kestrel used this sheet the last time a broadcast was also a mailbox. The top corner, with the figure, is gone. What is left:

The mailbox rides inside an ordinary broadcast. It is not the picture. Pell circled a channel and wrote VIDEO next to it. That channel is a ghost in this grab. Ignore Pell.

Each little section of the mailbox ends in a seal. If the seal does not match the page, the fade ruined it on the way down. Those pages are garbage even when the words look familiar. Drop them.

The addresses come first. The useful sentence is what the page was actually carrying, after the addresses.

The good pages were numbered by the crew in the order they were written. A later repeat is not a later page.

The margin also says “same nameplate as last night.” That note is about the letterhead you already read. It is not this mailbox.
