## Recovery

The recovery request went to Cerro Tololo (807). Two exposures of every planned field on 2041-10-15, 04:10 and 04:40 UTC. Their pipeline sends back only the things that moved between the two exposures, as tracklets (`recovery_tracklets.csv`). Positions are astrometric J2000. There are a lot of main-belt asteroids in that part of the sky.
