DR-ONE-Q rerun of DR-ONE's last flight, test hall B, flight 7
--------------------------------------------------------------

Flight 6 was the shakedown the day before (hand-held checks, then the stand). After it the FC board was swapped for the spare and screwed onto the tray the way the spare's connector reaches, not the way the drawing shows. Flight 6's file was deleted when flight 7 started.

Flight 7: power on, sat on the pad, lifted to hover height, small corrections, then leaned forward toward the hall doors for a couple of seconds as DR-ONE did before its last turn, came back level, turned left slowly and back, held. Then it rolled hard, went into the net and onto the floor. The battery came out on impact; nobody touched the card before it was imaged.

Quick look by the FC team:
- BBX0007.BIN ends in the hover, a few seconds before the roll. Nothing saturated in it.
- The last wobble before the end of the file is a negative pitch rate, nose coming up, so it was starting to tip toward the tail: S2.
- S2 bench-tested fine afterwards. So did S1 and S3. Nobody believes it.

Card imaged and sent up to the lander for the fault monitor to compare with DR-ONE's own codes.
