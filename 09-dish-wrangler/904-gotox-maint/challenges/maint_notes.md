# Drawer card — gotox-maint

The maintenance client from the motor controller. It runs on the bench machine. It does not phone home.

The night shift called the gesture “the unlock.” Wrong key, the tool stays locked and says so. Right key, it prints a token. That token is the thing the bridge wants. It is not written on this card, and it is not sitting in the tool as a sentence you can read with your eyes.

The laminated default, `MAINT-0000-0000`, is a joke from a previous crew. It has never worked. Neither has the station’s name, nor the stardate, nor Pell’s birthday.

The real key is assembled when the tool runs. Two numbers it already carries are stamped into a nameplate. Find the stamps. Do not guess the plate.

No other switch matters. The tool is small on purpose.
